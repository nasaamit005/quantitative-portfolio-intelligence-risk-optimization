{% elif active_page == "methodology" %}
RESEARCH DESIGN AND ASSUMPTIONS

Methodology and limitations

Definitions and assumptions behind the portfolio research environment.

Market data

The project uses a historical US equity universe of AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, AMZN, META, JPM, XOM, JNJ, COST, CAT and KO. The processed returns are based on adjusted market prices.

Return and covariance estimation

Expected returns use annualized historical arithmetic means. Covariance estimation uses the Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage estimator. The dashboard optimizer uses 252 trading days per year.

Portfolio optimization

The interactive optimizer supports maximum Sharpe ratio and minimum volatility objectives, with long-only fully invested portfolios and configurable asset weight limits.

Risk and validation

The research engine includes drawdown analysis, stress testing, sensitivity analysis, market regime analysis and walk-forward backtesting. These tools provide complementary views of risk.

Important research limitations
  • The selected equity universe has survivorship bias and is not a point-in-time historical universe.
  • Historical expected returns and covariance estimates are uncertain and can change materially across estimation windows.
  • Historical optimization results are not forecasts and do not guarantee future performance.
  • The dashboard optimizer is an in-sample research tool; use the separate walk-forward results to assess out-of-sample behavior.
  • Benchmark transaction-cost treatment may differ from the strategy cost model.
  • Market indices and yield indicators are not necessarily directly investable instruments.
