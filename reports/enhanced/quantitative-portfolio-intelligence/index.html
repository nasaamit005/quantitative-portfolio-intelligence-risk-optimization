Project Overview

Quantitative Portfolio Intelligence & Risk Optimization Platform brings portfolio construction, investment performance measurement and risk analysis into one integrated Python-based workflow. It applies quantitative finance techniques to historical US market data to compare portfolio strategies, evaluate investment outcomes and examine the trade-offs between return and risk.

Core Capabilities

Portfolio Optimization

Construct and compare equal-weight, minimum-volatility, maximum-Sharpe, hierarchical risk parity and risk-parity portfolios under defined investment constraints.

Backtesting & Performance

Evaluate historical portfolio performance, returns, volatility, drawdowns and the impact of transaction costs.

Risk Analytics

Examine portfolio risk through risk measures, drawdown analysis, market indicators and portfolio-level diagnostics.

Robustness & Sensitivity

Assess how strategy outcomes respond to changes in lookback periods, portfolio constraints and rebalancing frequencies.

Stress Testing

Investigate portfolio behaviour under adverse market scenarios and different market environments.

Walk-Forward Evaluation

Compare strategies using sequential historical evaluation to examine how portfolio decisions perform through time.

Research Dataset & Framework

The project uses historical US equity market data covering 12 selected equities, three market benchmarks and two market indicators. The configured research period is January 2015 through November 2026, subject to the available dataset.

12 Equities Selected US-listed stocks
3 Benchmarks Market comparison instruments
2 Indicators VIX and US Treasury yield
5 Strategies Portfolio construction approaches

Analytical Approach

From portfolio construction to investment evaluation

The platform combines constrained optimization, historical backtesting, risk measurement, transaction-cost analysis, sensitivity experiments, stress testing and walk-forward comparisons. Its objective is to make portfolio strategy evaluation more systematic, transparent and reproducible.
